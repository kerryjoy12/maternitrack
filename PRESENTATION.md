# MaterniTrack — Maternity Follow-Up Tracker

## Overview

MaterniTrack is a complete digital system for managing maternity care at **Itierio Nursing Home**. It replaces paper records and manual processes with a secure, web-based platform that connects the entire care team — from reception to delivery room to follow-up — and gives mothers direct access to their own care information.

---

## What It Does for Staff

| Area | How It Helps |
|------|-------------|
| **Patient Registration & Records** | One place to register, search, and view every patient. Full history — ANC visits, deliveries, postnatal care, labs, referrals — organised in a single timeline. No more lost files or duplicate paperwork. |
| **Antenatal Care (ANC)** | Record all ANC visits (1–4) digitally. Generate PDF visit summaries for mothers to carry. Track each patient's clinic stage and risk classification at a glance. |
| **Labour Monitoring (Partograph)** | Digital WHO-compliant partograph replaces the paper chart. Automatically alerts nurses and doctors when vital readings cross danger thresholds — catching complications early. |
| **Appointments** | Schedule, reschedule, mark attended or missed. See at a glance who is due for a visit and who has been missed. |
| **Postnatal & Child Health** | Track 7-day and 6-week postnatal visits. Record child growth, vaccinations (Kenya EPI schedule), and clinic visits — keeping mother and baby in one system. |
| **SMS Reminders** | Send automatic SMS reminders to mothers for ANC appointments, postnatal checks, and vaccinations. Choose English or Swahili templates. Drastically reduces no-shows. |
| **Clinical Alerts** | Critical lab results, partograph danger signs, and patient-reported symptoms appear instantly on the dashboard of the right staff member. Nothing falls through the cracks. |
| **Referrals** | Log referrals to other facilities with transport mode, urgency, clinical summary, and follow-up outcome. Track whether the referral was completed. |
| **Reporting & Audit** | Role-specific dashboards (admin, nurse, doctor) show real-time KPIs: active pregnancies, deliveries, missed appointments, high-risk patients. Full audit trail for every record change. |

---

## What It Does for Mothers

| Feature | Benefit |
|---------|---------|
| **Portal Access (Web & Mobile App)** | Mothers log in from their own phone to see their pregnancy week, EDD, and upcoming appointments. No need to call the clinic for basic information. |
| **Appointment Management** | View scheduled appointments and request rescheduling if needed — directly from their phone. |
| **Pregnancy Journey** | Follow their pregnancy week-by-week with ANC visit history, lab results, and growth milestones all in one place. |
| **Symptom Reporting** | Report symptoms (pain, bleeding, fever, etc.) directly to the clinic. The care team sees it immediately and can respond. |
| **Medical Records** | Access their own records and download ANC visit PDFs — empowering mothers with their health information. |
| **Learning Resources** | Educational articles on pregnancy, nutrition, danger signs, and baby care — curated for each stage of pregnancy. |
| **SMS Appointment Reminders** | Receive appointment reminders via SMS in English or Swahili. Reduces missed visits and improves maternal outcomes. |

---

## The Problem It Solves

- **Lost or misfiled paper records** — all data is stored securely, searchable in seconds
- **Missed appointments** — SMS reminders and digital scheduling reduce no-shows significantly
- **Delayed complication detection** — real-time alerts for danger signs during labour, high-risk pregnancies, and abnormal symptoms
- **No visibility for mothers** — the portal keeps mothers informed and engaged in their care
- **Manual reporting** — dashboards auto-generate, saving hours of weekly reporting work
- **Fragmented care** — ANC, delivery, postnatal, child health, and referrals are linked in one patient record
